    SPHERExLens · Documentation

    Pipeline Overview & Reference

    Detects moving and variable infrared sources across multi-epoch SPHEREx cutouts via ZOGY optimal image subtraction.

    Instrument

    NASA's SPHEREx mission surveys the sky repeatedly across 102 spectral channels (0.75–5.0 µm).

    Pipeline Steps

    1. Ingest: Query IRSA SIA2 for SPHEREx Level-2 cutouts and cross-match catalogs (Gaia DR3, AllWISE, VSX).
    2. Co-add: Apply bitmask flags and stack exposures per epoch onto a common tangent-plane grid.
    3. Subtract: Run ZOGY optimal subtraction between epoch pairs to produce difference (D) and significance (S) images.
    4. Triage: Cross-match detections >5σ, verify proper-motion kinematics and NEOWISE history, and assign a verdict category.
    5. Validate: Run synthetic source injection-recovery across SNR and proper motion (μ) bins.

    Map Legend

    • KNOWN Matched to a catalogued star, variable, or mover.
    • CANDIDATE No catalog match within radius.
    • TARGET Primary field target position.
    1. Select Field: Pick a registered target from the toolbar dropdown or enter custom RA / Dec in degrees and click Load field from IRSA.
    2. Inspect Sky Viewer: Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click markers to populate the Triage Verdict panel.
    3. Switch Layers: Toggle between Co-add, Difference D, and Significance S, or switch to All-sky view.
    4. Replay Snapshots: In the Sky Viewer toolbar, choose a previous published version from Snapshot, or press Play to step oldest to latest. Select a candidate or known object first to follow its dipole/monopole with the purple trail.
    5. Utility Windows: Open History, Statistics, or Recovery from the toolbar as draggable floating panels.
